03

How to read this portfolio

Each design has one job. These are the terms used on the cards, in plain English.

📰Market news

Goal: explain a market event in a quick, easy post. Aim: be informed and visible.

🏆Corporate

Goal: show awards, launches, meetings and events. Aim: build trust.

🚀Promo design

Goal: ask people to act, like joining a channel or trying an app. Aim: action.

🕊️Awareness

Goal: show solidarity or draw attention to an important cause. Aim: stand with the audience.

🌙Special day

Goal: greet the audience on national or religious days. Aim: community connection.

📐Format

Square suits the feed, tall gives room for detail, wide suits banners. Pick by where it will be shown.

🎯Visual metaphor

An image that explains the idea without words, like a handshake for a partnership. One idea per design.

✍️Headline hierarchy

Big words first, small words last. The post should be understood in two seconds.

05

What the designs tell us

Patterns across all 104 social media designs.

01One look per client

Each client keeps its logo, colours and layout across every post, so followers recognise the brand in one glance, even on a busy feed.

02A repeatable news format

Most of the 27 news designs share one look: a big headline, one strong scene and a rising or falling arrow where it helps. This makes production fast and keeps the feed consistent.

03Real photos where trust matters

Awards, meetings, branch openings and sessions use real photos. Illustrations are used where news has no photo.

04Two languages, one look

Urdu and Arabic calligraphy sit beside English text on religious and Shariah designs, with the same brand frame.

Recommended next steps
Add motion

Turn the strongest news designs into short animated clips for Reels and Stories.

Build carousels

Split longer topics, like policy news, across 3 to 5 swipeable slides.

Create Urdu versions

Offer news explainers in Urdu to reach a wider audience.

06

How we make every design

A repeatable process that keeps the quality consistent from one post to the next.

STEP 01BriefWe confirm the topic, audience and the one message.
STEP 02CopyThe headline and supporting lines are written short and clear.
STEP 03ConceptWe choose the visual idea: photo, scene, chart or icons.
STEP 04LayoutLogo, colours and type are applied in the brand style.
STEP 05ReviewThe client checks the facts, spelling and tone.
STEP 06DeliveryFiles are exported in the right size for each platform.
